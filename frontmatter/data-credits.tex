\chapter*{Data Credits}

Every chart in this book is redrawn from the datasets below by a script of the
book's repository, which also holds the small tables the charts are drawn from.
Each dataset keeps its own licence; the book's licence covers only the book's
own text, drawings and scripts. Where a dataset's licence asks for the original
sources to be cited, they are listed in that dataset's documentation, and the
caption of each chart names its dataset.

\begin{itemize}\small
\item \textbf{Maddison Project Database}, version 2023. Bolt, J. and J. L. van
  Zanden (2024), ``Maddison style estimates of the evolution of the world
  economy: A new 2023 update'', \textit{Journal of Economic Surveys}, 1--41.
  Licence CC BY 4.0. The original estimates behind each country's series are
  listed in the database's source sheet.
\item \textbf{Penn World Table}, version 11.0. Feenstra, R. C., R. Inklaar and
  M. P. Timmer (2015), ``The Next Generation of the Penn World Table'',
  \textit{American Economic Review}, 105(10), 3150--3182. Licence CC BY 4.0.
\item \textbf{The World Bank}: World Development Indicators (November 2026
  release) and Poverty and Inequality Platform: Percentiles (2021 PPP). Licence
  CC BY 4.0; the original source of each indicator is named in its metadata.
\item \textbf{Jord\`a--Schularick--Taylor Macrohistory Database}, release 6.
  Jord\`a, \`O., M. Schularick and A. M. Taylor (2017), ``Macrofinancial History
  and the New Business Cycle Facts'', \textit{NBER Macroeconomics Annual 2016},
  vol.~31. Licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
\item \textbf{Gapminder}: life expectancy, child mortality, fertility,
  population and income per person, used for the years before 1950. Based on
  free material from GAPMINDER.ORG, CC-BY LICENSE.
\item \textbf{Eurostat}: harmonised consumer prices, unemployment, government
  deficit and debt, national accounts, job vacancies, income inequality, life
  satisfaction, business demography. Source: Eurostat; licence CC BY 4.0.
\item \textbf{U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics}: Consumer Price Index for All
  Urban Consumers, all items and item indexes. Public domain.
\item \textbf{Community Emissions Data System} (CEDS), version 2025\_03\_18,
  doi:10.5281/zenodo.15059443. Licence CC BY 4.0.
\item \textbf{U.S. Energy Information Administration}: international energy
  statistics. Public domain.
\item \textbf{FAO}: FAOSTAT, Crops and livestock products. Licence CC BY 4.0.
\item \textbf{CEPII Gravity database}, version 202211. Conte, M., P. Cotterlaz
  and T. Mayer (2022), ``The CEPII Gravity database'', CEPII Working Paper
  2022-05. Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0.
\item \textbf{National Supported Work samples} as distributed by R. Dehejia
  (CC BY-NC). LaLonde, R. (1986), \textit{American Economic Review}, 76,
  604--620; Dehejia, R. and S. Wahba (1999), \textit{Journal of the American
  Statistical Association}, 94(448), 1053--1062; Dehejia, R. and S. Wahba
  (2002), \textit{Review of Economics and Statistics}, 84, 151--161.
\item \textbf{U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics}: Average Price Data; Job Openings and
  Labor Turnover Survey; Consumer Expenditure Surveys, tables by quintile of income. Public domain.
\item \textbf{Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System}: statistical releases H.15
  (selected interest rates) and H.4.1 (factors affecting reserve balances). Public domain.
\item \textbf{European Central Bank}: Eurosystem consolidated balance sheet, total assets.
  Source: ECB. Numbers computed from it are ours.
\item \textbf{Datasets bundled with statsmodels} (public domain, as their
  documentation states), each with its own source note: U.S. macroeconomic
  quarterly data (\texttt{macrodata}), Grunfeld's investment data, the RAND
  Health Insurance Experiment extract, Engel's food expenditure data, and others
  where a chapter names them.
\end{itemize}
