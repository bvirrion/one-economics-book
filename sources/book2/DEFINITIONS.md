**Sync update.** Removed (Book 1 owns; Book 2 recalls in prose, no definition pair): correlation, natural experiment,
unit-elastic demand, law of one price, real GDP, consumer price index, marginal and average tax rate, barrier to entry,
natural monopoly, disposable income, hyperinflation, capital per worker, average annual growth rate, patent, import quota,
currency appreciation and depreciation. Also Book 1's (do not define): necessity, luxury, demand/supply function, shock,
trade balance/surplus/deficit, price level, budget deficit/surplus, public debt, interest rate, real wage, real/nominal value,
labour productivity, output per worker, intermediate and final good, income tax, payroll tax, tax on purchases, network
effect, institution, planned/market economy, contract enforcement, entrepreneur, sunk cost, gains from trade, property
right, shortage, market surplus, price floor/ceiling, tax per unit, subsidy, exchange rate, basket, base year, inflation
rate, deflation, growth rate, compound growth, doubling time. The "Contested" table below is kept as the Phase A record; the
rulings settle it.

Every term Book 2 will define with `\emph{term}\index{term}` inside a `definition`, its owner chapter and its label. Labels are
`def:<prefix>:<chapter-slug>:<name>`. A model this book owns is indexed in its `model` box (marked *model*). Terms Book 1
owns (outline ownership map) are **not** here: Book 2 recalls them in prose. Contested terms are flagged **C** and listed at the
end for the sync.

| Term | Book 2's proposal | Why contested |
|---|---|---|
| correlation; natural experiment | g10 `economic-models` | Book 1 g9 `institutions-and-prosperity` says "a correlation across countries is not yet a cause" and uses the three natural experiments: if Book 1 defines either word, Book 2 recalls it. Book 3 (Year 1 statistics) owns the correlation **coefficient**. |
| excess demand, excess supply | g10 `market-equilibrium` | Book 1 g7 owns *shortage* and *surplus*. Book 2 defines the quantities Q^d − Q^s and Q^s − Q^d as the algebraic versions and avoids the bare word *surplus* in that sense (it collides with consumer surplus). |
| unit-elastic demand | g10 `elasticities` | Book 1 g9 owns elastic and inelastic demand; if it also defines unit-elastic, Book 2 drops it. |
| real interest rate | g10 `price-indices` | The ownership map gives it to g12 `present-value`, but g10 `price-indices` uses it two years earlier (the outline's own description), and Book 1 g8 has "the interest rate after inflation (first look)". Rule 4 (earliest chapter) points to Book 1 g8 if it defines it, else g10. |
| minimum wage | g11 `labour-market` | Book 1 g9 `price-controls-taxes-subsidies` may define a minimum wage as its price-floor example. |
| Lorenz curve, Gini coefficient | g11 `inequality` | The map gives them to g11; Book 1 g9 `income-distribution` must not define them (only shares by fifths). |
