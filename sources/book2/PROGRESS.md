## For the shared docs

- **JST R6, USA, `unemp` 2019 = 2.0** (WDI SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS gives 3.67; JST's GBR/DEU/FRA/JPN 2019 values look right). A chart
  using JST US unemployment must stop in 2018 or splice WDI for 2019.
- **NSW `cps_controls` earnings are top-coded at 25,243.6** (1982 dollars): 2,427 of the prime-age men sit at the cap, so mean
  earnings by schooling are flat and cannot stand for the return to schooling.
- **JST `narrowm` for the USA behaves like the monetary base** (847 in 2007, 1,669 in 2008, 5,207 in 2020), so it serves for a
  central-bank-money chart; say "narrow money as the database defines it" in the source line.
- **WDI US expenditure shares (NE.*.ZS) start in 1970**, not 1960.
- **`maddison_regions` World row**: 21 years only (as its docstring says).
- **Eurostat HICP computers**: CP09131 (personal computers) starts in 1996 for France only (Germany 2015, euro area 2017);
  CP0913 (information processing equipment) covers the euro area from 1996.
- **WDI TM.TAX.MRCH.WM.AR.ZS, USA 2019 = 13.8** against 1.5–4.0 in every other year: an outlier to leave out (g12-13 drops
  the USA from its tariff chart).
- **JST CPI inflation differs from BLS** (US 1980: JST 11.2 %, BLS annual average 13.5 %). Use one source per chapter;
  g12-10 and g12-12 use BLS.
- **`pwt()` frames: `p.pop` is the DataFrame method**, not the population column; write `p["pop"]`.
- **Eurostat `ilc_pw01`** (life satisfaction): filter `isced11 == "TOTAL"`, `sex == "T"`, `age == "Y_GE16"`; 2018 has the
  most countries (38 national codes, Greece as EL, the UK as UK, Kosovo as XK).
- **Eurostat `une_rt_q`**: `time` is a string like `2009-Q1`, and the series starts in 2009.
- **PWT `avh` across countries** barely tracks income (correlation −0.18 over 130 countries, 2019); over time within rich
  countries it falls clearly. Use the time series for "free time rises with income".
- **pgfplots in `omscatter`**: the style sets `only marks`, so a fitted line read from a table needs `sharp plot` or it is
  invisible; connected scatter (Phillips loops) likewise.
- **Tables from CSVs**: the book's default `1000 sep={\,}` prints years as "2 015"; give year columns `1000 sep={}`.
- **Paper abstracts**: PNAS returns 403 and PubMed a cookie wall to the fetcher; the Europe PMC REST search
  (`ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:<pmid>&resultType=core&format=json`) returns the abstract.
  IDEAS pages for JEL/JME articles often carry no abstract.
- **Term links, Book 2**: "long run"/"short run" are harvested from the firm's fixed-input definition and would land in the
  grade-12 macro chapters in the wrong sense: STOP them. "shares" in "shares the risk" is a verb: protected.
- **pandas `row.corr`, `row.le`, `row.pop`** resolve to methods (or nothing) on a Series row: three chart tests failed silently
  on `k.corr > x`. Use `k["corr"]`.
- **WDI life expectancy, CAF** jumps by twenty years from one year to the next (31.5 in 2019, 18.8 in 2022): leave it out.
- **CEDS CO2 per person** has absurd values for small territories (Faroe Islands 0.01 t): keep countries of a million people
  or more.
- **Commons API returns HTTP 429** after a few quick searches, and thumbnail downloads too: one request at a time with
  pauses, and never write a file from a failed request (a stale buffer once saved one photograph under another's name).
