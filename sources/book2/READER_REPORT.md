## Patterns across the book

1. ***Tested* fields that hold evidence merely consistent with the model, without saying so.**
   - This is the most frequent defect: 18 of the 114.
   - It occurs in every grade: R2-002, 003, 006, 009, 017, 018, 027, 030, 039, 043, 045, 048, 057, 059, 062, 074, 088, 104.
   - CONTRIBUTING asks for the words "consistent with". A single sweep of every `\tested` line in the book would settle most
     of them.
2. **The `omscatter` reference-line trap** (R2-065, R2-080, R2-114).
   - A line drawn with `\addplot[...] {x}` inside an `omscatter` axis prints only its two end marks.
   - The trap is already known for fitted lines (PROGRESS, "For the shared docs"), but it was missed for 45-degree lines.
3. **Book 1 re-taught instead of recalled.** R2-012 (g9-01 equilibrium algebra), R2-023 (g8-02 value-added chain), R2-024
   (necessity and luxury) and R2-060 (the rule of 72 against Book 1's rule of 70).
4. **People named outside the two allowed forms:** R2-054 (Ford), R2-072 (Young), R2-077 (Harrison), R2-097 (Volcker),
   R2-109 (Easterlin in a model box).
5. **Log and times-ten axes.**
   - Grade 10 and 11 times-ten axes have ticks off the powers of ten: R2-026 and R2-038.
   - Grade 12 log axes are not named in their captions: R2-107 and R2-108.
6. **Labels clipped or overprinted in diagrams:** R2-005, 010, 020, 044, 049, 064, 075, 079, 087, 100.
