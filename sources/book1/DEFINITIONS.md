# Book 1 (Grades 6–9) — defined terms (Phase A, 2026-11-20; frozen for Book 2 at the sync)

Every term Book 1 will define with `\emph{term}\index{term}` inside a `definition`, its owner chapter and its label.
Labels: `def:<prefix>:<chapter-slug>:<name>`. One owner per notion across Books 1–2 (outline's "Notion-ownership map").
Terms marked **(contested)** are discussed at the end; the sync's ruling replaces the mark.

Book 2 recalls these in prose ("as the grades 6–9 volume showed") and does not redefine them.

## Sync rulings (2026-11-20, `SERIES_DEFINITIONS.md` R2)

All twelve proposals below were confirmed, with these additions: g9 also owns *unit-elastic*; g8 also owns *average
annual growth rate*; the *real interest rate* is owned by Book 2 g10 `price-indices` (Book 1 says it in words only);
*share (of a total)* and *depreciation (of a currency)* carry their qualifiers in `\index{}` (Book 2 owns *share (in a
company)* and *depreciation (of capital)*), and the bare words go to `STOP` in Phase C.

## Contested terms (as proposed at Phase A)

1. **export, import** — the map gives "exports, imports, tariff" to g9 `international-trade`, but g6 `a-connected-world`
   owns "trade as a share of output" and g7 `comparative-advantage` opens on an exports chart. Proposal: g6 owns
   *export* and *import*; g9 owns *trade balance*, *trade surplus*, *trade deficit*, *tariff*, *import quota*.
2. **market surplus** — g7 owns "shortage and surplus" (excess supply). Book 2 owns *consumer surplus* and *producer
   surplus*, and Book 1 g8 owns *budget surplus*. Proposal: Book 1 defines the qualified *market surplus* (and writes
   "surplus" alone only in that sense in g7–g9), *budget surplus* in g8; the bare word goes to `STOP` in Book 1's term
   config.
3. **consumer price index** — g8 owns "price index, inflation rate, real and nominal"; g10 `price-indices` recalls and adds.
   Proposal: Book 1 g8 defines *consumer price index*; Book 2 g10 defines *Laspeyres index*, *GDP deflator*, chained
   indices.
4. **average tax rate, marginal tax rate** — in no map. g8 needs both for Brenland's tax bands. Book 2 g11 owns *marginal
   cost/revenue*; Year 1 `labour-supply-and-saving` will use the marginal tax rate with calculus. Proposal: Book 1 g8
   owns both (earliest chapter, rule 4); Books 3–5 may re-found.
5. **monopoly, natural monopoly** — the map gives "competition and monopoly, in words" to g8 and recalls it in g11
   `monopoly`. Proposal: g8 owns *competition*, *monopoly*, *barrier to entry*, *natural monopoly*; g11 defines the
   formal objects (*perfect competition*, *market power*, the monopoly's profit-maximising rule) without redefining
   *monopoly*.
6. **price elasticity of demand** — g9 owns "elasticity as a ratio of percentages"; g10 owns "point and arc elasticities".
   Proposal: g9 owns the term *price elasticity of demand* (and *elastic*, *inelastic*); g10 defines *arc elasticity*,
   *point elasticity*, *income elasticity*, *cross-price elasticity*, *price elasticity of supply*.
7. **real interest rate** — g8 `banks-saving-interest` describes "the interest rate after inflation (first look)"; the
   map gives *real interest rate* to g12 `present-value`. Proposal: g8 writes it in words with no definition pair.
8. **wage** — g7 `households` (income source) vs g8 `jobs-wages-unemployment`. Proposal: g7 owns *wage*; g8 owns the
   labour-market terms; Book 2 g11 owns *human capital*, *minimum wage*.
9. **correlation** — "a correlation across countries is not yet a cause" (g9-06). The mathematics series defines
   *correlation* only in `bachelor-1/25-two-variable-functions` (harvested 2026-11-20), so g9-06 defines it in words
   (rule 4, earliest chapter). Book 2 g10
   `economic-models` owns "establishing a cause" (the notion), and the statistical *correlation coefficient* belongs to
   the mathematics books or Year 1.
10. **income** — g7 `households` defines *income* (of a household); g6 `living-standards` defines *income per person*
    (the national average). Proposal: both, as written; Book 2 `national-accounts` owns *national income*.
11. **capital** — g8 owns *capital* (physical); Book 2 g11 owns *human capital*. Book 1 never uses the bare word for
    money or a capital city in g8–g9 prose without qualification; `STOP`/`EXTRA_PROTECT` in Phase C.
12. **Engel's law** — not in any map; owned by g7 `households` (the food-share notion is g6's). Book 2 g10
    `elasticities` uses income elasticity and may name the law without a definition pair.
