## Overall judgement

All 36 chapters were read, with their solution files. No `sources/PAUSE` appeared.

**Counts:** 85 defects. 7 major (R5-014, 016, 021, 027, 030, 048, 072; three of them, 016, 021 and 027, are major
for thinness), 42 minor, 36 nit.

**The arithmetic is sound.** Every exercise and problem question was recomputed, about 1,300 answers in all. Only
one final answer is wrong (R5-014, ch. 7 ex. 7). A handful of intermediate lines are wrong (R5-008, 009) or rest on
data the problem never printed (R5-023, 025, 041, 056). The ledger and figdata numbers checked (well over a third
per chapter) match their CSVs and rows, apart from the specific items logged.

**Part by part:**

- **Econometrics (ch. 1--6): the strongest part.** It is long, derived and worked, with real listings and staged
  failures. Its defects are local: a reversed parallel-trends sentence (R5-009), a listing the text misdescribes
  (R5-006), CPS top-coded earnings used where PSID was the rule (R5-007), and one *tested* field that cites a
  simulation (R5-004).
- **Trade (ch. 7--11): good in ch. 7, 9 and 10; thin in ch. 8 (R5-016) and ch. 11 (R5-021).** Ch. 8 promises
  "seventy years" of tests and starts in 1987. Ch. 11 has no WTO institutions and one chart. There is one wrong
  answer (R5-014).
- **International macro (ch. 12--14): sound but borderline thin in ch. 12--13.** The charts need captions on
  coverage and war years (R5-022, R5-024).
- **Money (ch. 15--16): the weakest teaching in the first half.** Ch. 15 defines tools it never uses (R5-027).
  Ch. 16's money–inflation regression is mislabelled as log–log, and its chart hides a point and the equality line
  (R5-030). Fractional year ticks run through both chapters (R5-028).
- **Public economics (ch. 17--22): the thinnest stretch of the book.** Ch. 18--21 run 7--8 pages each, with no
  listing and with their real-data charts dropped. Ch. 19 is the thinnest (R5-043). Ch. 21's Meltzer–Richard
  "test" is partly mechanical reverse causation (R5-048, major).
- **Labour (ch. 23--26): correct but uniformly borderline.** Typical faults: back-filled PWT hours (R5-055), charts
  that clip or misplace their fit (R5-057, R5-063), and the gender pay gap absent from ch. 25.
- **IO (ch. 27--30): clean mathematics** (the auction, deferred-acceptance, two-sided and Averch–Johnson problems
  all re-solved). Every chapter is about 6.5--8 pages with no listing and its multi-study chart dropped, so the
  records are thin (kidney exchange, business dynamism, cartels).
- **Growth and development (ch. 31--36): the best-evidenced part.** It has rich records, graded debates, and
  independent tests (closed forms, hand decompositions). Its defects are about measurement: Levitt's corrected
  police estimate presented as settled (R5-072, major), Gapminder fill-ins drawn as data (R5-075), endpoint noise
  in an annual Malthusian series (R5-076), mixed income and consumption Ginis in the welfare measure (R5-083), and
  a closing-table claim that contradicts ch. 24 (R5-084).

**Patterns, in order of weight:**

1. **Thin chapters after ch. 15.** Almost every chapter from 16 to 30 is 6.5--9 pages with no body listing. The
   missing material is usually the chart or record that PROGRESS logs as dropped for lack of a source. Restoring
   the logged charts would fix ch. 18, 20, 21, 26, 27, 28 and 29 in large part. Ch. 11, 15 and 19 need new
   sections, not only charts.
2. ***Tested* fields that cite a measurement, a simulation, a list of markets or nothing:** R5-004, 017, 026, 051,
   058, 068, and the one-review Hotelling field in ch. 35.
3. **Charts that hide or misplace data:** clipping (R5-050, 057, 063, 046); a legend over the only moving stretch
   (R5-073); a rounded tick style on a 0--1 axis (R5-065); fractional year ticks (R5-028); diagram vertices off
   the curve (R5-034, 059); fill-ins drawn as data (R5-075).
4. **Back-filled or spliced series:** PWT hours (R5-055), Gapminder TFR (R5-075). The choice of PWT `rgdpe` for
   growth is a related point (R5-071).
5. **Weak test design:** format-only or line-count tests in ch. 10, 16, 17, 21, 23, 24 and part of 26 (R5-020).
   Ch. 22, 25, 26, 27, 30--32, 34 and 35 have good, independent tests. A sweep found no pandas attribute access colliding
   with a DataFrame method in any test.
6. **Terms defined and never used:** R5-042, 053, 064, 068.
7. **Problems that use unprinted or contradictory data:** R5-023, 025, 041, 056.
8. **Neutrality is generally good.** Debates give positions on both sides with grades, and government failure
   sits next to market failure throughout. The lapses are one-sided records: R5-054, 061, 081, and R5-072 (a
   finding presented without its published correction).

**Nothing forbidden was found in print** (no school of thought, no curriculum or university, nothing personal).
The datasets used (CEDS, Gapminder pre-1950, Eurostat ilc_pw01, PIP) are on the approved list.
