# Book 5 (University Year 3, `b3`) --- defined terms

Every term Book 5 plans to define with an `\emph{term}\index{term}` pair inside a `definition`, its owner chapter and the
definition's label (`def:b3:<chapter-slug>:<name>`; several terms may share one definition). Phase A, 2026-11-20; updated after the sync
(2026-11-20) per `sources/SERIES_DEFINITIONS.md` R1 and R3 and the coordinator's rulings for Book 5. Dropped and now
recalled in prose: seigniorage, hyperinflation, markup, intensive/extensive margins, old-age dependency ratio,
replacement rate, revenue equivalence (proved as a proposition), first-/second-price and common-value auctions, IPV,
collusion/tacit collusion, law of one price, quota rent, specification search, excess burden (prose synonym of Y1's
deadweight loss).

Flags: **R** re-founded school notion (allowed by the outline's table "From school to university"); **C** contested ---
another book or series may own it, ruling needed at the sync (see the report). Everything else is new to the series in
Book 5. Terms Book 5 only **recalls** are listed in `BRIEFS.md`, Appendix C.

## Count and term-config notes

About 250 terms in 190 definitions. Words that need `STOP`/`EXTRA_PROTECT` in Phase C (one sense per word): *instrument*
(also a policy instrument), *panel*, *placebo*, *bandwidth*, *running*, *cutoff*, *treatment*, *complier*,
*specification*, *replication*, *gravity*, *quota*, *peg*, *floor* (floor system vs price floor of the school volumes),
*corridor*, *reserves* (bank reserves vs foreign reserves), *standard* (emission standard vs gold standard vs standard
error), *markup*, *market* (relevant market), *cartel*, *patent*, *tipping*, *threshold*, *margin* (intensive margin vs
profit margin vs margin of error), *premium* (skill premium vs term premium vs risk premium), *trap* (poverty trap vs
liquidity trap), *transition*, *dividend*, *rent* (scarcity rent vs rent seeking vs housing rent), *Ramsey* (two
distinct definitions here and Book 4's model).
