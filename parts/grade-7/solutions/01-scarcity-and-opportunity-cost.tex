\begin{solution}{exo:g7:scarcity-and-opportunity-cost:7}

(b) Cooking: $6 + 12 = 18$ dalens; ready meal: 14 dalens. The ready meal is cheaper.

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g7:scarcity-and-opportunity-cost:10}

wheat costs $40 \div 5 = 8$ homes.
\end{solution}
