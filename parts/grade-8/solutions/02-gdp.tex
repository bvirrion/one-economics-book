\begin{solution}{exo:g8:gdp:9}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:gdp:10}
(a) The home dinner adds 12 dalens (the food bought). The restaurant dinner adds 40 dalens: 12 of
food and 28 of \omterm{def:g8:gdp:value-added}{value added} by the restaurant. (b) Not necessarily: the dinner is the same; the
family's own cooking was left out of GDP, so the rise of 28 dalens partly replaces work that was
done but not counted.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g8:gdp:11}
(a) Farm $300$; bakery $900 - 300 = 600$; GDP $= 900$ dalens. (b) \omterm{def:g7:households:wage}{Wages} $200 + 450 = 650$, \omterm{def:g7:firms:profit}{profits}
$100 + 150 = 250$; GDP $= 900$ dalens. (c) \omterm{def:g7:households:household}{Households}' spending on bread: 900 dalens.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:g8:gdp:1}
\textbf{1.} Orchard 40 and glass works 20 million dalens (they buy no \omterm{def:g8:gdp:intermediate-good}{intermediate goods}).
\textbf{2.} $90 - 40 - 20 = 30$ million dalens.
\textbf{3.} $150 - 90 = 60$ million dalens.
\textbf{4.} $40 + 20 + 30 + 60 = 150$ million dalens: exactly what \omterm{def:g7:households:household}{households} spent on juice.
\textbf{5.} $225\,014 + 88\,484 + 78\,653 + 129\,777 - 128\,664 = 393\,264$ million dalens.
\textbf{6.} $129\,777 - 128\,664 = 1\,113$ million dalens.
\textbf{7.} \omterm{def:g6:a-connected-world:import}{Imports} are part of what \omterm{def:g7:households:household}{households}, \omterm{def:g7:firms:firm}{firms} and the government bought ($C$, $I$, $G$),
but they were produced abroad; GDP counts only what is produced in Brenland.
\textbf{8.} Consumption.
\textbf{9.} $C$: $225\,014 \div 393\,264 \approx 57.2\,\%$; $I$: $88\,484 \div 393\,264 \approx
22.5\,\%$; $G$: $78\,653 \div 393\,264 \approx 20.0\,\%$.
\textbf{10.} $379\,562\,000\,000 \div 9\,235\,000 \approx 41\,100$ dalens.
\textbf{11.} $393\,264\,000\,000 \div 9\,291\,000 \approx 42\,327$ dalens.
\textbf{12.} $(42\,327 - 41\,100) \div 41\,100 = 1\,227 \div 41\,100 \approx 0.030$, a rise of
$3.0\,\%$. No: about $1.7$ points of it come from higher \omterm{def:g7:money:price}{prices}, so the quantity of goods per
person rose by only about $3.0 - 1.7 = 1.3\,\%$.
\end{solution}
