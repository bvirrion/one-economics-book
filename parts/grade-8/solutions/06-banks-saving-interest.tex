\begin{solution}{pb:g8:banks-saving-interest:1}
\textbf{1.} $5\,000 \times 0.03 = 150$ dalens.
\textbf{2.} $5\,000 + 150 \times 10 = 6\,500$ dalens.
\textbf{3.} $5\,000 \times 1.03^{2} = 5\,304.50$; $5\,000 \times 1.03^{10} \approx 6\,719.58$ dalens.
\textbf{4.} $6\,719.58 - 6\,500 = 219.58$ dalens.
\textbf{5.} $8\,000 - 5\,000 = 3\,000$ dalens; $3\,000 \times 1.06^{2} = 3\,370.80$ dalens.
\textbf{6.} $3\,000 \times 1.09^{2} = 3\,564.30$ dalens.
\textbf{7.} $3\,564.30 - 3\,370.80 = 193.50$ dalens.
\textbf{8.} The second \omterm{def:g8:banks-saving-interest:bank}{bank} may judge him more likely to \omterm{def:g8:banks-saving-interest:default}{default} (for example, it knows less about his
\omterm{def:g7:households:income}{income}), or it may have higher costs.
\textbf{9.} $(127.7 - 115.9) \div 115.9 = 11.8 \div 115.9 \approx 10.2\,\%$.
\textbf{10.} $5.42 - 10.2 = -4.78$ points. Negative: a lender at the central \omterm{def:g8:banks-saving-interest:bank}{bank}'s rate got back less,
in goods, than he lent.
\textbf{11.} $(143.5 - 140.0) \div 140.0 = 0.025$: $2.5\,\%$. Rate minus inflation: $4.62 - 2.5 =
2.12$ points.
\textbf{12.} \omterm{def:g7:money:price}{Prices} are multiplied by $1.025^{10} \approx 1.280$. The \omterm{def:g8:banks-saving-interest:deposit}{deposit} is worth $6\,719.58 \div
1.280 \approx 5\,250$ dalens in the \omterm{def:g7:money:price}{prices} of the start: more than the $5\,000$ deposited, so her
\omterm{def:g7:households:saving}{saving} gained about $250$ dalens' worth of goods.
\end{solution}
